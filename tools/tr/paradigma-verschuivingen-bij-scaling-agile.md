TITLE: Paradigm shifts in Scaling Agile
![paradigm.png](/images/blog/paradigm.png)

We should all know by now that applying Agile principles and Scrum as an empirical framework for IT trajectories or projects already brings various paradigm shifts with it. Still, I am always disappointed by what comes of it in practice. Continuing to think in the existing ways of working and ways of governing remains a stubborn thing.

Now, scaling up agile working enterprise-wide is a (for me lasting) trend. But the number of paradigm shifts involved is many times greater still.

In this blog I give a (non-exhaustive) overview of paradigm shifts that an organization has to go through when scaling up agile working. I do not explain them, but the intention is to trigger employees and management of organizations that are busy with, or want to start with, scaling up agile working, and to make them aware of the challenges they face.

Again, the list is not exhaustive, and the ‘shifts’ within a category are also in random order. I am someone who likes to formulate things sharply, and to exaggerate. This is because in practice I notice that many of the necessary changes mentioned are simply not acknowledged, seen, implemented, or are even stubbornly blocked, by decades of ingrained and accumulated habits and ways of thinking. In this context I like to refer to a video by former colleague Daan Kalmeijer: <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TJAugy1Tbeo> .

I hope the list provides food for discussion, and if there are any questions I can of course be reached.
